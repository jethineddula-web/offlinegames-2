Play I Am Not A Robot Free Online

I Am Not A Robot is two hundred levels of increasingly unreasonable “prove you are human” checks. It opens as a joke about captchas and becomes a real lateral-thinking puzzle somewhere around level forty, once the prompts stop making sense on purpose. Progress saves in your browser, so you can walk away and come back to it. Expect to be annoyed and to carry on anyway.

How to play I Am Not A Robot in your browser

Solve CAPTCHA Tests: Complete 200 handcrafted levels ranging from grid image selection ("click all crosswalks") to absurd pattern-recognition and timing challenges.

Earn Diamonds: Collect diamonds for every successfully cleared prompt to build your stash.

Equip Power-Ups: Spend your hard-earned diamonds in the shop on essential verification tools:

Skips: Jump past tricky or near-impossible security prompts.

Reveals: Highlight correct grid answers automatically.

Shields: Protect your streak against trick questions and wrong clicks.

Boosts: Multiply diamond payouts for future levels.

About this free browser game

Two hundred levels, with a level-select screen so you can go back to whichever one beat you. Winning rounds earns diamonds, which can be spent in an in-game shop on hints, skips and reveals, and there are optional rewarded video ads if you would rather watch one than wait. The joke is aimed at anyone who has failed a captcha three times running and started to doubt themselves.

I Am Not A Robot is designed for the spare 5 minutes, not a 2-hour session. It does have an optional in-game shop for hints and skips, paid for with what you win rather than with money.
