How to play Stellar Escape in your browser

Pilot the Craft: Steer your orange vector ship through narrow gaps in floating space rocks using responsive thrust controls.

Reach the Gateway: Navigate toward the glowing blue escape portal to clear the level before your fuel runs out.

Avoid Collisions: Dodge polygonal asteroids of varying sizes and movement speeds—a single impact destroys your ship.

Utilize Shields: Grab temporary blue energy bubbles scattered across the starfield to absorb hits and push past hazardous zones.

About this free browser game

Vector shapes on a black background in the tradition of Asteroids, with neon picked out on the ship and the debris around it. Steering carries inertia, and that is the whole difficulty — it asks less for fast reflexes than for starting your turn before you think you need to. Short runs, immediate restarts.
