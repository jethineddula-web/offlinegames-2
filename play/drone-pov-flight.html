Play Drone POV Flight Free Online

Drone POV Flight is first-person drone racing through twelve maps, from a sunset downtown to a glacier bay and a midnight city. You fly the whole thing from the camera on the nose, which is the point and also the difficulty — there is no outside view to save you, so the first few gates are usually a lesson in how much a small stick input actually does. Races are timed runs through air gates, with crystals to pick up and targets to hit along the way. Earnings go into a hangar of six airframes and a set of upgrades, and progress saves in the browser.

How to play Drone POV Flight in your browser

Steer: Move the mouse to look and steer, or use A and D to turn and bank. On a phone, hold the screen and drag.

Climb and Dive: W and S pitch the nose up and down. Q and E roll the drone level again when a turn leaves you sideways.

Boost: Hold SPACE for speed. Boost drains and refills, so save it for straight sections between gates.

Fly the Gates: Pass through the ring gates in order against the clock. Missing one costs you far more time than slowing down for it would have.

Collect and Shoot: Pick up crystals in the air and press F to fire at targets. Both add to what you earn from a run.

Upgrade: Spend crystals in the hangar on a faster airframe, motors, batteries, guards or an ability on R. Press ESC to pause.

About this free browser game

Twelve maps, each with its own weather and time of day, and three stars per map to chase if you want a reason to fly one again. The hangar sells six drones and a row of upgrades with crystals earned from flying, and there are optional rewarded ads if you would rather watch one than grind for the next airframe. It runs on Three.js, hosted here rather than pulled from anyone else's CDN, so it keeps working with the connection off. Landscape only on a phone — it asks you to turn the handset sideways before it starts.

What it is like to play

Drone POV Flight is designed for the spare 5 minutes, not a 2-hour session. No daily rewards, no energy system, no shop that sells power. You open it, play a round, close it. High score saves locally, so you can beat your own best next time without an account.
