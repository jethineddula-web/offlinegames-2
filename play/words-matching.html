Play Words Matching Free Online

Words Matching puts a wheel of letters at the bottom of the screen and a crossword grid above it. You swipe through the wheel to form a word, and if it fits the grid the tiles fill in. The satisfying part is finding the long word first and watching half the board resolve at once. The dictionary ships with the game, so it keeps working with the connection switched off.

How to play Words Matching in your browser

Connect Letters: Swipe or tap across the circular letter wheel at the bottom to connect letters in order and form valid words.

Fill the Grid: Correctly spelled words automatically populate the matching letter tiles at the top.

Earn Gems: Complete levels and discover bonus words to collect valuable blue gems.

Clear the Level: Find all required target words on the board to unlock the next puzzle stage.
