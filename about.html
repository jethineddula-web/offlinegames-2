who we are

About Offline Games

OfflineGames is a free, family-friendly library of pick-up-and-play browser games, built and maintained by Jethin Productions. Everything runs in your browser tab — nothing to install, no account to create, and no personal information required to play.

Each cartridge is picked for the same reasons: fast to load, easy to pick up, and actually fun once you get a feel for it.

We keep the site intentionally simple. If something breaks, the Contact page is the fastest way to reach us.

How the vault is run

This is not a scrape of other game portals. Files live in our own games/ folder. When we add Color Clash, Lodo, or Juicy Match, we add the build and a line in the catalog so a phone and a laptop read the same list. We do not hide games behind an account, and we do not sell player profiles — we do not collect them.

Editorial standards are ordinary: a game has to start, it has to be understandable without a wiki, and it has to be something we would play in a spare ten minutes. Strategy guides are in Guides, and rights for every title are on the credits page.

Family use

Colouring, match-three, word puzzles and board games sit in the same grid as a zombie shooter and a hard arcade run. Calling all of that "family-friendly" without qualification would be dishonest, so every cartridge carries a content rating — Everyone, Everyone 10+ or Teen — shown on the cover in the grid and on each game's own page.

The ratings are ours, not an official board's, and we apply them conservatively: anything on a boundary goes in the higher tier. Which games suit which ages, and exactly what we do and don't collect, is set out in our guide for parents.

If you need a page taken down, write through Contact and say which cartridge. Rights and attribution for every title are listed on the credits page.

Who writes and builds this

Jethin Productions is an indie studio based in Hong Kong, building small HTML5 games since 2023. The founder is a web developer and parent who wanted a games site without pop-up hell, login walls, or a 30-second unskippable ad in front of a 30-second game. Every cartridge in the vault is coded here, not scraped from other portals. Art, sound, and level design are original or licensed under CC0 / SIL Open Font License (see credits).

You can reach us anytime at jethinproductions@gmail.com or through the contact form — we reply within 2 days. The guides — Sudoku, 2048, chess openings, and more — come from playing these games ourselves. They cover strategy, how the site is built, and picks for parents and teachers; see Guides.

Every game in the vault gets tested on Android WebView, iOS Safari, and desktop Chrome before it goes live. Games are served straight from offlinegames.art/games/ — this domain, not a third-party iframe — over HTTPS, with no chat features and no loot boxes.
