Privacy Policy – Offline Games Data Safety

Last updated: 18 September 2026.

What we collect

OfflineGames does not require an account to play. We do not knowingly collect personal information such as your name or email address unless you choose to send it to us directly (for example, by emailing us through the Contact page). High scores and favorites stay in your browser. Standard, non-identifying technical data may be collected automatically by our hosting provider.

Advertising and cookies

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.

You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info.

More detail on how Google uses information from sites that use its services is at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Who is responsible for your data

OfflineGames.art is operated by Jethin Productions, an independent studio based in Hong Kong. For the purposes of the UK and EU General Data Protection Regulation, Jethin Productions is the data controller for this site. You can reach us at jethinproductions@gmail.com about anything on this page.

Consent, and visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

If you are visiting from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, a consent message is shown before any personalised advertising cookie is set. You can accept, refuse, or choose which purposes you allow, and you can change that decision at any time by reopening the privacy options link in the site footer.

Refusing consent does not stop you playing. Every game on this site works the same way with or without advertising cookies; you will simply see non-personalised ads instead.

Legal bases for processing (GDPR)

Where the GDPR applies, we rely on the following legal bases:

  • Consent (Article 6(1)(a)) — for personalised advertising cookies and any similar identifier set by Google. This is the basis we rely on for advertising in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and you may withdraw it at any time.
  • Legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)) — for the short-lived server logs our hosting provider keeps in order to serve pages, stop abuse and diagnose faults.

Your rights under the GDPR

If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland you have the right to request access to any personal data we hold about you, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict or object to how it is processed, to receive it in a portable form, and to withdraw consent at any time without affecting processing that already happened.

In practice we hold very little: this site has no accounts, no analytics of our own, and no visitor database, so in most cases there is nothing to export or delete beyond what your own browser stores and what you can clear yourself. If you would like to make a request, email jethinproductions@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days. You also have the right to complain to your national data protection authority; in the UK that is the Information Commissioner's Office.

Advertising data collected by Google is held by Google as a separate controller. Requests about that data should go to Google directly, through Google's privacy policy.

California residents (CCPA/CPRA)

If you are a California resident you have the right to know what personal information is collected about you and for what purpose, to request deletion of it, to correct inaccurate information, and to opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising. You also have the right not to be treated differently for exercising any of these rights, and this site does not do so.

We do not sell personal information for money. However, serving personalised ads through Google may count as “sharing” under California law. To opt out, use the privacy options link in the site footer, or turn off ad personalisation in Google Ads Settings. A Global Privacy Control signal sent by your browser is also honoured.

To make a request under California law, email jethinproductions@gmail.com with “California privacy request” in the subject line.

Your choices

You can clear or block cookies in your browser settings at any time, and blocking them does not stop the games working. You can reopen the privacy options link in the footer to change your advertising choices, opt out of personalised ads at Google Ads Settings, or opt out of many vendors at once at aboutads.info and youronlinechoices.eu.

Game content

All games are built by Jethin Productions and served from this domain. We do not embed games from third-party portals. Advertising is the only third-party content on this site.

Children and families

Parts of this site are written for children and for the adults choosing games for them — our guide to games for under-8s is an example — so we treat those pages as directed to children and handle them more carefully than the rest of the site.

On pages aimed at children we request non-personalised advertising only. No advertising profile is built from a child's visit, and no interest-based targeting is applied there.

Across the whole site we do not ask for a name, an email address, an age or a photograph, there is no account to create, no chat, nothing players write is ever shown to other players, and no way for a child to communicate with anyone through the site. High scores and saved positions never leave the device.

In line with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information — for example by emailing us through the Contact page — write to jethinproductions@gmail.com and we will delete it promptly. Parents may also ask what has been received and refuse any further collection.

Game reviews

After you have played a game for a while, we may ask you to rate it. Answering is optional, and you can tell us not to ask again for that game.

If you send a review, we receive the game's name, your star rating, anything you chose to write, the approximate time you had spent in the game, whether you were on a phone or a computer, and your browser language. We do not receive your name, email address, account or location. Before a review leaves your device, anything that looks like an email address, phone number or web link is removed from the text.

Reviews are stored in a Google Form owned by Jethin Productions, under Google's privacy policy, and are used only to decide which games to improve and build next. They are never published on this site or shown to other players. To have a review deleted, email jethinproductions@gmail.com with the game name and roughly when you sent it.

The notification bell, the "Continue playing" list and game suggestions are worked out entirely in your own browser from what you have played. That information never leaves your device.

Data retention

We do not run a database of visitors. High scores and favourites are stored by your own browser and never reach us. Server access logs kept by our hosting provider are retained for a short period for security and troubleshooting.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to jethinproductions@gmail.com, or through the Contact page.
