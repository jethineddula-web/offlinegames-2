News & Vault Updates

OfflineGames.art is updated every week. New games arrive, existing ones are refined, and the offline experience is tested against the kind of connection people actually have rather than a perfect one. This page is the public record of those changes, newest first.

Every game is hosted here on offlinegames.art and plays in the browser with no download and no account. See About for who builds the site, or Guides for strategy writing.

Recent updates

  • 13 September — Games now refresh as soon as an update is published. Some phones had been holding on to an older copy for several days; the vault checks for a newer version whenever you are online, and still plays offline once a game has loaded.
  • 12 September — New guides published, covering 8-ball positioning and spin, the tic-tac-toe never-lose tree, picks for short sessions, games for children under eight, the history of Ludo and chess, and classroom ideas for teachers.
  • 11 September — Eight games added: Arrows Puzzle, Bees Escape, Math Runner, Words Matching, Numberly, Thunder-Circuit, Save The Dog and Helicopter Simulator 3D. Each arrives with its own page, a content rating and notes on offline play.
  • 9 September — Every cover now carries a content rating: Everyone, Everyone 10+ or Teen. Ratings are applied conservatively, so anything on a boundary goes into the higher tier. The guide for parents explains what each one means in practice.
  • 8 September — Accessibility fix: pinch-to-zoom is no longer disabled on the written pages, so About, Guides, Privacy and the articles can be zoomed and read comfortably.

How a game reaches the vault

Every title is built and hosted here rather than embedded from another portal. Before a game goes live it is played on an Android phone, an iPhone and a desktop browser, given a content rating, and checked with the connection switched off, so we know exactly how much of it survives without a signal.

Nothing is locked behind an account, and no player profile is created or sold. What little is stored — a high score, a saved position — stays in your own browser. The Privacy page sets this out in full, and rights and attribution for every title are listed on Credits.

Found a problem, or want to suggest a game? Contact us — we reply within two days.
