Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026.

What we collect

OfflineGames does not require an account to play. We do not knowingly collect personal information such as your name or email address unless you choose to send it to us directly (for example, by emailing us through the Contact page). High scores and favorites stay in your browser. Standard, non-identifying technical data may be collected automatically by our hosting provider.

Advertising and cookies

This site may display ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings.

Third-party game content

Some games on this site are embedded from or link to third-party sources. Those pages may set their own cookies or collect data under their own privacy terms, which we do not control.

Children's privacy

This site is family-friendly, but it is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to our contact form, or use the Contact page.
