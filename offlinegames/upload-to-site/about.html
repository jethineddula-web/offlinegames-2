who we are

About OfflineGames

OfflineGames is a free, family-friendly library of pick-up-and-play browser games, built and maintained by Jethin Productions. Everything runs in your browser tab — nothing to install, no account to create, and no personal information required to play.

Each cartridge is picked for the same reasons: fast to load, easy to pick up, and actually fun once you get a feel for it.

We keep the site intentionally simple. If something breaks, the Contact page is the fastest way to reach us.

How the vault is run

This is not a scrape of other game portals. Files live in our own games/ folder. When we add UNO, LUDO, or Juicy Match, we add the build and a line in the catalog so a phone and a laptop read the same list. We do not hide games behind an account, and we do not sell player profiles — we do not collect them.

Editorial standards are ordinary: a game has to start, it has to be understandable without a wiki, and it has to be something we would play in a spare ten minutes. Longer notes live on the library page. The practical how-to is in Guides.

Family use

Coloring, match-three, word puzzles, and board games sit in the same grid as a zombie shooter and a hard arcade run. We label categories so you can filter. We still expect a parent to glance at the cover. If you need a page taken down, write through Contact and say which cartridge.

See every title on the library page, or head back to the vault and pick a cartridge.
