How to play

1

Pick a crayon

The tray at the bottom is your palette. Each crayon has a number and a color.

2

Drag each matching pixel

Find that same number in the picture and drag across every square. Each pixel colors on its own — not the whole patch.

3

Finish the picture

When a crayon is used up it fades. Keep going until every pixel is filled. Zero mistakes earns three stars.

Stuck? Hint fills a small leftover cluster. Peek flashes the finished picture. Fill color paints every remaining pixel of the crayon you picked.
