UMBRA FIGHT
Fifty gates. One shadow.

Open index.html in a browser (a local server is best if ads are blocked on file://).

Controls
  A / D     move
  W         jump
  S         block
  J         punch
  K         kick
  L         special
  Esc       pause

Touch buttons appear on phones.

Ads (AdSense)
  Banner under menus
  Watch an offering for shadow coins
  Extra life after a fall
  +40 HP when you are nearly down

Sound can be toggled from the title screen.
Progress saves in this browser.
