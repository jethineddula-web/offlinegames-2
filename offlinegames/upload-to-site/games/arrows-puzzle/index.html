How to Play

1
Tap an arrow and it flies in the direction it points until it leaves the board.
2
If it crashes into another arrow on the way out, you lose one of your 3 lives.
3
Clear every arrow off the board to win. Finish with lives left for 3 stars.
4
Stuck? Use a hint to light up an arrow that is safe to shoot right now.
