BeeEscape

Rex wandered into the wrong garden. Fetch his bones and get him home.

How to play

Move — drag anywhere on screen, or use arrow keys / WASD.
Collect every bone. The doghouse only opens once Rex has them all.
Bees sting anything that strays too close — you don't have to touch one. The red ring is its reach, and an angry bee will dart at you.
Flower beds are safe — bees refuse to fly over them.
Honey is sticky and slows Rex down. Wind pushes him sideways.
Spray cans scare every bee away for six seconds.
