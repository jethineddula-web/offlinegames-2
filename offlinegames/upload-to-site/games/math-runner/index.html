HOW TO PLAY

1. Steer. Drag anywhere, or use ← → / A D on a keyboard.

2. Choose gates. Two gates come at you every time. Green ones (×, +) grow your number, red ones (÷, −) shrink it. Pick the better side.

3. Dodge. Bombs, spike traps, saw blades and walls all cut your number down. Watch the sky too — birds drop bombs that leave burning craters. A Shield boost blocks one hit.

4. Grab diamonds ◆. Spend them in the Shop on rockets, hats, boosts and an extra launcher. Bird bombs can also blast nearby traps away for you!

5. Blast the robot. Your final number becomes launcher ammo. Tap when the aim bar is in the yellow zone for a CRITICAL hit.

One robot, 200 plates. The same giant robot follows you through the whole game. Every level you clear rips one armour plate off its core — one hit will never destroy it. Clear all 200 and the core finally blows.

200 levels, each one different. Good luck.
