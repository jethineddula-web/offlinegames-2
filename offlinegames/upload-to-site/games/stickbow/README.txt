STICKBOW
========

A 50-loft stick-figure archery game. An NPC tosses apples up from the ground.
Drag to draw the bow, follow the dotted line, and hit them before they fall.

HOW TO PLAY
- Drag on the range to draw. Drag length is power. Move up or down to aim.
- A dotted line traces the shot. The ring on an apple is a predicted hit.
- Release to loose. Limited arrows per loft — reach the mark to advance.
- Later lofts toss faster, smaller, windier, and more apples at once.
- Out of arrows? Watch a short ad for two extra shots.

HOSTING
Upload this folder (index.html, game.css, game.js) to any static host.
Open index.html locally for a quick test.

ORIENTATION
The game locks the orientation you start a loft in (when the browser allows),
and keeps a playable layout if a device still rotates.

MUTE
Use the speaker button. Preference is saved on the device.
