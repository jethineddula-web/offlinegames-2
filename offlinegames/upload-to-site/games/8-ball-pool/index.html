THE PLAYERS’ CLUB

How to play

01

Choose your match

Play the House for a game against the bot, or use Pass & Play for two people on one device.

02

Aim and shoot

When the table is still, drag from the cue ball toward the line of your shot. Pull farther for more power and release to strike.

03

Claim your set

The first coloured ball pocketed assigns solids or stripes. Pocket one of your own balls to continue your turn.

04

Finish cleanly

Clear your group before the 8-ball. A scratch passes the turn. Pocketing the 8-ball early, or on a scratch, loses.

Sound: select a match or press Sound: On once to allow browser audio, then cue, collision, rail, and pocket sounds will play.
