Save My Dog — Bee Rescue

A free browser puzzle game. A swarm of bees closes in on a puppy from every direction and you have one job: draw walls with your finger or mouse and keep him alive until the timer runs out. Ink is limited, so the tidier your shelter, the more stars you earn.

How to play

  1. Drag on the screen to draw a wall. Start and finish a line on the ground or on scenery so it is braced — loose lines get shoved aside by the swarm.
  2. Press GO to release the bees, then survive the countdown. You can keep drawing while they attack if you still have ink.
  3. Watch the puppy as well as the bees. He has weight, so he can be pushed off a ledge, and lava and deep water will finish him.

What you are up against

Ordinary bees, fast bees, bulky bees that shift whole walls, chewers that eat through ink, swervers, splitters, ghosts that slip through a single layer, bomb bees, and ants that march in along the floor. One hundred levels, each with a different arena, swarm and hazard.

Plays in any modern browser on phone, tablet and desktop. No download and no sign-up.
