CONTROLS

Keyboard (PC)

  • E — start the engine
  • W / S — raise / lower the helicopter (collective)
  • A / D — turn left / turn right on the spot (pedals)
  • ↑ / ↓ — fly forward / backward
  • ← / → — slide left / slide right
  • C camera · H hint · R restart · P pause

Touch

  • Left stick — cyclic (tilt to move)
  • Right slider — collective (lift)
  • ⟲ ⟳ buttons — pedals (turn on the spot)
  • 🎥 button — switch between the three views

How to fly

  • Hold W until you lift off, then let go — the helicopter holds its height.
  • Hold ↑ to lean forward and pick up speed. A helicopter moves by leaning.
  • A / D spin you on the spot to aim; the arrows do the travelling.
  • Ease off W and level out before touching down — under 5 m/s.
  • Touching a building destroys you. The rotor is wider than the body — give the towers plenty of room.

Helicopter Simulator 3D — free online helicopter flying game

Helicopter Simulator 3D puts you in the pilot seat of a fully modelled rotorcraft above a living city of skyscrapers, avenues, moving traffic, parks and rooftop helipads. The flight model uses a real collective, cyclic and anti-torque pedals, so the machine leans to move and settles into a hover exactly the way a helicopter should. Every blade spins, every rooftop casts a shadow, and every one of the hundred missions asks something different of you.

Features

  • 100 missions — ten hand-designed set-pieces followed by ninety generated city runs with a rising difficulty curve
  • Three camera views: cockpit pilot view, medium chase view and a wide cinematic view
  • Authentic controls — collective, cyclic and tail-rotor pedals, with an optional hover assist for newcomers
  • A real city: glass towers, brick apartments, roads with traffic, street lights, parks and rooftop landing pads
  • Weather and time of day — clear mornings, golden sunsets, city lights at night, fog banks and storm fronts
  • A hangar of five aircraft and five upgrade lines: turbine power, rotor head, aerodynamics, fuel cells and airframe plating
  • Runs in the browser on desktop and mobile — full touch controls, no download, no install

How to play

Press E to start the engine and let the rotor spool up. Raise the collective with W until the skids leave the pad, then ease it back to hold a hover. Tilt the cyclic forward with the up arrow to accelerate, bank with the left and right arrows, and use A and D on the pedals to swing the nose around. Collect every marker, thread the rings and — when the mission asks for it — set down gently on the pad. Watch your fuel and keep the rotor clear of the towers.
