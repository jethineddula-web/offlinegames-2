How to play

  • Fill the grid so every row, every column, and every 3×3 box contains the digits 1 through 9 exactly once.
  • Tap a cell to select it, then tap a number below to place it. Bold numbers were given to you and can't be changed.
  • Turn on Notes to pencil in small candidate numbers while you think.
  • Hint reveals the correct number for the selected cell after a short ad — use as many as you like.
  • Three wrong entries end the round, so double-check before you tap.
