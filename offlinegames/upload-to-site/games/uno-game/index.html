How to play

Normal

Match the discard by color or number. Wilds play anytime and call a color. Skip, Reverse, +2, Wild, and Wild +4 do what they say. Two players: Reverse acts as Skip.

If you cannot play, draw one. You may play that card if it fits. Press UNO when you drop to one card — miss it and you draw two.

Tasks

A table task is always live — play the card it names and a chaos event hits the table. Hands can be shuffled and dealt back out, swapped with the smallest (or biggest) hand, rotated around the table, or you may have to keep drawing until you hit a number or color.

There is no private mission. Everyone sees the same task. +2 and +4 still stack.

Friends

Same device hides hands between turns. Host a table and share the four-letter code — anyone with this game on the web joins that table.
