Original rhythm

Aurora Keys

Tiles drop from the top of three lanes — A S D, or ← ↓ →. Tap the small ones. Hold the long ones.

How to play

  1. Tap the small tiles

    Black tiles fall down three lanes. Tap a tile as soon as you can — you do not have to wait until it is halfway.

  2. Empty lanes hurt

    Tapping a lane with no crystal costs a life. You have three.

  3. Hold the long ones

    Press and hold long sliding tiles from the moment they appear until they pass the line.

  4. It never ends

    A run lasts until you lose your lives. The longer you last, the faster tiles fall.

  5. Prism Burst

    Fill the meter with combos, then tap Burst (or Space) to clear the field.

  6. Keyboard

    Lanes are A S D or ← ↓ →. Esc pauses.
