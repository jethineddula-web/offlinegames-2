PIANO WORLD - SAME-FOLDER BACKGROUND MUSIC

Place these six MP3 files in the SAME folder as piano-world.js and
piano-world.html (or index.html). Do not create an audio/ subfolder.

  ordinary.mp3    -> Sunny Meadows
  midnight.mp3    -> Midnight City
  tokyo.mp3       -> Neon Tokyo
  phonk-gym.mp3   -> Inferno Gym
  villain.mp3     -> Villain Castle
  showtime.mp3    -> Showtime Streets

Suggested folder layout:

  piano-world.html
  piano-world.css
  piano-world.js
  sw.js
  ordinary.mp3
  midnight.mp3
  tokyo.mp3
  phonk-gym.mp3
  villain.mp3
  showtime.mp3
  images/

In this source project, put the MP3s directly in public/.

Use the exact lower-case filenames, including .mp3. Changing a file's
extension does not convert it to MP3. If your files use M4A, OGG or WAV,
change CONFIG.audioSources in piano-world.js and the SONGS list in sw.js.

The music plays at normal speed and loops throughout each endless run.
The tiles move faster, not the audio. There are no generated substitutes.
Missing files can be added later; the game also offers Play without music.

Tiles fall at double speed. Missing a tile or tapping a lane with no tile
costs a heart (after the first 10 seconds). A run continues until all 3 rhythm hearts are lost,
and allows unlimited free new runs. There is no song-length timeout or
five-play energy limit. A rewarded ad may revive the current run, but is
not required to start a new one.

Hosted offline playback requires one online visit and successful caching
of the game and actual MP3 files. Browser storage limits can affect caching.
Ads require an internet connection. Local audio selection in Settings
also works without the network, but only lasts for that session.

The recordings are not included. Add audio you own or have permission to use.
