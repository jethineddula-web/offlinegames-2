Credits, Rights & Licences – Free Browser Games

Every game listed below is built and hosted by Jethin Productions and served from offlinegames.art. We do not frame games from other portals, we do not hotlink other operators' builds, and there is no third-party game content on this site.

We publish this page because "trust us" is not good enough on a games site. If you want to check a claim, every game's files are under offlinegames.art/games/ on this domain — you can look.

Traditional and public-domain games

Several cartridges implement games whose rules are traditional or public domain: chess, sudoku, ludo, tic-tac-toe, eight-ball pool, and colour-and-number shedding card games. Rules are not copyrightable. The code, artwork, sound and presentation in our versions are our own work.

Where a traditional game has a well-known commercial brand attached to it, we deliberately do not use that brand. Our shedding card game is called Color Clash, not the trademarked name; our aviation runner is Sky Dodge. If you spot a title on this site that still reads like somebody's trademark, please tell us — we will rename it.

Third-party components

The site itself uses no JavaScript frameworks and no bundler. The only third-party resources loaded on a page are:

  • Google Fonts — Chakra Petch and IBM Plex Sans, both under the SIL Open Font License.
  • Google AdSense — advertising. See the privacy page.

Individual game builds may use open-source libraries under their own licences. Helicopter Simulator 3D, for example, runs on hand-written WebGL2 with no dependencies. Where a library is used, its licence text ships alongside the build.

Takedown and attribution requests

If you believe something on this site infringes your rights, write to jethinproductions@gmail.com or use the contact page. Please include:

  • The exact page or cartridge name.
  • What you believe is infringing and what work it infringes.
  • How we can reach you.

We will take the item down while we look into it, rather than leaving it up and arguing. That is the whole policy.
